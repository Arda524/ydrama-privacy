Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-12

This Privacy Policy explains how Ydrama collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Account information such as email address and password when you sign in using Firebase Authentication.
  • Device information such as device type, operating system version, and app version.
  • Push notification information such as Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token and notification preferences.
  • App usage information such as selected categories, saved preferences, and local app settings.
  • Content and links data stored in Firebase Firestore and local storage.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Allow sign-in and account access.
  • Deliver app content, updates, and notifications.
  • Store and manage user preferences.
  • Improve app reliability and user experience.
  • Respond to support requests and maintenance needs.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services such as:

  • Firebase Authentication for sign-in and account management.
  • Firebase Firestore for storing app content and related data.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for push notifications.
  • Google Play services and Android system services required for app functionality.

These services may process information according to their own privacy terms and policies.

4. Local Storage

The app may store information locally on your device to improve functionality, such as selected preferences and lightweight caching. This local data is stored on the device and is not sold to third parties.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only when necessary to:

  • Provide core app functionality through Firebase and related service providers.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
  • Protect the security and integrity of our services.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no digital system is completely risk-free.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict specific processing activities.

If you want to exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Contact Us

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the app and on our website with the updated date.
